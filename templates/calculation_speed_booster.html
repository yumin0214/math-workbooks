Mental Math Foundations

Addition, subtraction & multiplication shortcuts
📖
Concept Note
Mental math means calculating in your head — no pen, no calculator! The trick is to break numbers into easy parts. For addition, round up to the nearest 10 first. For subtraction, think of the difference between numbers. For multiplication, use doubling, halving, or breaking numbers apart. With daily practice, your brain learns these shortcuts automatically!
✏️
Worked Examples
Example 1 — Addition
48 + 37 = ?
Step 1: 48 + 2 = 50
Step 2: 37 − 2 = 35
Step 3: 50 + 35 = 85
Answer: 85 ✓
Example 2 — Multiply by 9
7 × 9 = ?
Trick: 7×10 = 70
Then: 70 − 7 = 63
(×10 minus one set)
Answer: 63 ✓

⚡ Multiply by 5 in a Flash!
To multiply any number by 5 — just multiply by 10 and divide by 2. Twice as easy!
48 × 5 = ?
Step 1: 48 × 10 = 480
Step 2: 480 ÷ 2 = 240 ✓

Try: 36 × 5 = 360 ÷ 2 = 180 ✓

Multiplication Tables & Patterns

Tables 1–20, square numbers & quick recognition tricks
📖
Concept Note
Tables are the foundation of all fast calculation. If you know your tables up to 20 by heart, you can solve most problems in seconds! Notice patterns: tables of 5 always end in 0 or 5. Tables of 9 have digits that always add up to 9. Square numbers (1, 4, 9, 16, 25...) follow a beautiful pattern — n×n. Spot these patterns and your brain retrieves answers instantly!
✏️
Worked Examples
Example 1 — Table of 15
15 × 7 = ?
15 = 10 + 5
10×7 = 70
5×7 = 35
70+35 = 105
Answer: 105 ✓
Example 2 — Square Number
14² = ?
14² = (10+4)²
= 100+80+16
= 196
Answer: 196 ✓

Trick of the Day
⚡ Table of 11 for 2-digit numbers!
For 11 × any 2-digit number: add the two digits and put the result in the middle!
11 × 35 → 3+5=8 → 385 ✓
11 × 72 → 7+2=9 → 792 ✓
(If sum ≥10, carry the 1 left!)

Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

Conversion speed & quick estimation
📖
Concept Note
Fractions, decimals, and percentages are three ways of saying the same thing. Know these by memory: 1/4=0.25=25%, 1/2=0.5=50%, 3/4=0.75=75%. For percentages, break it down: 10% is dividing by 10, then build from there — 30%=3×10%, 15%=10%+5%. Estimation = get close fast by rounding numbers!
✏️
Worked Examples
Example 1 — % Shortcut
35% of 80 = ?
10% of 80 = 8
30% = 3×8 = 24
5% = 8÷2 = 4
35% = 24+4 = 28
Answer: 28 ✓
Example 2 — Fraction to %
3/8 as % = ?
1/8 = 12.5%
so 3/8 = 3×12.5%
= 37.5%
Answer: 37.5% ✓

Trick of the Day
⚡ Flip the numbers for easy %!
x% of y = y% of x. If a % looks hard, just swap the numbers!
8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 8÷4 = 2 ✓
4% of 75 = 75% of 4 = 3 ✓

Algebra Speed Drills

Simple equations, substitution & quick solving
📖
Concept Note
Algebra is just arithmetic with a mystery number! When you see x+5=12, ask: "What do I add to 5 to get 12?" — the answer is 7. For 3x=24, ask: "3 times what gives 24?" — that's 8. Isolate the variable by doing the opposite operation on both sides. Practice enough and you'll solve simple equations in under 5 seconds!
✏️
Worked Examples
Example 1 — Simple Equation
4x + 7 = 31
4x = 31−7 = 24
x = 24÷4 = 6
Check: 4(6)+7=31 ✓
Answer: x = 6 ✓
Example 2 — Substitution
a=3, b=5: 2a+3b
= 2(3)+3(5)
= 6+15 = 21
Answer: 21 ✓

Trick of the Day
⚡ Balance Method — Picture a Weighing Scale!
Think of an equation as a scale. Whatever you do to one side, do exactly the same to the other side.
5x − 10 = 20
Add 10 both sides: 5x = 30
Divide by 5: x = 6 ✓
